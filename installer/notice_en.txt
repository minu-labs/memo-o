1. Free software
MemoO is a free offline recording + speech-to-text (STT) program.
This software is provided "as is", and the author is not responsible for any results of its use.

2. Privacy
MemoO never connects to the internet. Recordings and transcripts are processed and stored
only on this PC and are never sent to an external server. Data location: %LOCALAPPDATA%\MemoO

3. Legal notice on recording
Laws on recording conversations differ by country and region. Some places allow you to record
a conversation you take part in, while others require everyone's consent. Secretly recording
conversations you are not part of is illegal in most places. Check the laws where you live
before recording. You are responsible for how you use the recording feature and its results.
